## 1. Funnel Objective

Convert public attention into real business outcomes:
- clicks
- enquiries
- purchases
- bookings
- qualified leads

---

## 2. Funnel Structure

```text
Awareness
  ↓
Interest
  ↓
Trust
  ↓
Offer Consideration
  ↓
Action
  ↓
Sale / Booking / Access Grant
```

---

## 3. Awareness Stage

### Entry points
- TikTok
- YouTube Shorts
- X
- Instagram Reels
- Google Business profile
- website home page

### Goal
Make the person stop scrolling and understand why this matters.

### Messaging
- practical pain points
- relatable situations
- value-focused hooks

---

## 4. Interest Stage

### Entry points
- product page
- service page
- educational post
- brand landing page

### Goal
Get the user to believe the offer is relevant.

### Messaging
- explain the problem clearly
- tell them what result they can expect
- showcase the process

---

## 5. Trust Stage

### Entry points
- reviews
- case studies
- digital proof
- Google Business listing
- clear service explanation

### Goal
Reduce uncertainty.

### Messaging
- show process
- show expertise
- show real work
- remove fake claims

---

## 6. Offer Consideration Stage

### Entry points
- offers page
- WhatsApp contact
- buy buttons
- pricing cards

### Goal
Make the next step obvious.

### Messaging
- clearly priced offer
- clear included outcome
- minimal friction

---

## 7. Action Stage

### Entry points
- Paystack checkout
- direct WhatsApp enquiry
- service booking

### Goal
Complete the transaction or action.

### Messaging
- confirm what happens next
- show follow-up steps clearly

---

## 8. Conversion Logic by Channel

### TikTok / Shorts
- hook → offer CTA → website or WhatsApp

### YouTube
- educational value → trust → offer or booked consult

### Google Business
- local discovery → contact or website visit

### Website
- interest → trust → purchase or enquiry

---

## 9. Revenue Conversion Tracking

Every funnel step should be measurable:
- views
- clicks
- contact rate
- offer click rate
- purchase conversion rate
- average order value

---

**This funnel is the engine that turns attention into verified business result.**
