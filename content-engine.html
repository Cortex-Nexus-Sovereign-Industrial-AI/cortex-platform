Starter curriculum

Four lessons to begin with.

Each unit has a learning objective and a small task. Finish one before increasing complexity.

LESSON 01 · SYSTEMS THINKING

Map one everyday process

Objective: Identify the steps and weak points in a familiar process.

  1. Choose a process, such as recording a sale or preparing a study session.
  2. List inputs, steps, expected output and who is responsible.
  3. Mark one possible failure point and suggest a simple check.

Evidence of learning: A one-page process map with one improvement.

LESSON 02 · AI FOUNDATIONS

Write a well-framed AI request

Objective: Understand how context, constraints and output format shape a useful answer.

  1. State the task and who the result is for.
  2. Give relevant context and limits; never include passwords or private customer data.
  3. Ask for a clear format and verify important facts before using the result.

Evidence of learning: A before-and-after prompt with a note about the difference.

LESSON 03 · DIAGNOSTIC REASONING

Separate observations from assumptions

Objective: Avoid jumping to a cause before checking evidence.

  1. Describe a symptom without guessing its cause.
  2. List two plausible explanations.
  3. Identify a safe check that could help distinguish between them.

Evidence of learning: Observation → hypotheses → test → result → next step. Hazardous electrical work should be left to appropriately qualified people.

LESSON 04 · PRACTICAL OPERATIONS

Create a record that supports a decision

Objective: Turn scattered information into a usable record.

  1. Choose a workflow such as stock, enquiries or tasks.
  2. Define only the fields needed for a decision.
  3. Use sample data, check for gaps and write one conclusion the record supports.

Evidence of learning: A small table plus one decision and its reason.

Quality rule: Accuracy, safe practice and clear documentation matter more than speed. No lesson should imply guaranteed income, credentials or outcomes.
