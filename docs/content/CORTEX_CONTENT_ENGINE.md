# Cortex Intelligence Nexus — Content & Education Engine

**Status:** Layer 5 active  
**Date:** 2026-10-06

## Purpose
Create a repeatable education and content system that turns Cortex and DARKTRONIX practical knowledge into structured public learning, demonstrations, documentation, coaching and professional knowledge products.

## Canonical public surface
- Content engine: https://cortex-platforms.netlify.app/content-engine.html

- Learning Lab: https://cortex-platforms.netlify.app/learning-lab.html
- DARKTRONIX: https://cortex-platforms.netlify.app/darktronix.html

## Four content streams
1. AI & digital systems
2. Technical diagnostics
3. Learning & capability
4. Real-world operations

## Publishing sequence
Teach → Demonstrate → Document → Engage → Convert responsibly.

## Quality gate
Every content unit should have a clear learning objective, practical value, accurate framing and an intentional next step. Do not invent testimonials, audience metrics, certifications or customer results.

## Conversion boundary
Free education can lead to coaching, technical review, consulting and knowledge products. Commercial offers must remain truthful and proportional to demonstrated capability.

## Relationship to DARKTRONIX
DARKTRONIX 9V LABS is the technical education and practical-skills division. Its physical laboratory history supplies real-world experience; the public direction is education-first.
