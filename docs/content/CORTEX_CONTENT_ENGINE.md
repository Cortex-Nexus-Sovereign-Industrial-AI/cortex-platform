## Four learning streams
1. **AI & Digital Systems** — AI foundations, clear prompts, workflow design, responsible automation and human oversight.
2. **Systems Thinking** — inputs, processes, outputs, dependencies, failure points and process improvement.
3. **Technical Diagnostics** — observation, fault isolation, measurement logic and evidence-led reasoning through DARKTRONIX educational examples.
4. **Real-World Operations** — inventory, trader workflows, logistics, record keeping and small-business automation.

## Learning method
Frame the question → Learn the principle → Practise deliberately → Check the result → Document and apply.

## Starter lessons now presented on the public page
- Map an everyday process; submit a one-page process map and one improvement.
- Write a well-framed AI request; compare a before-and-after prompt and verify important facts.
- Separate diagnostic observations from assumptions; document hypotheses, safe checks and results.
- Create a practical record that supports a decision; use sample data and explain the conclusion.

Each lesson has a defined objective and evidence of learning. Technical or electrical activities must remain within safe and appropriate skill boundaries. Younger learners need age-appropriate guidance and supervision where appropriate.

## Publishing sequence
Teach → Demonstrate → Document → Engage → Convert responsibly.

## Quality and trust gate
Every content unit needs a clear learning objective, practical value, accurate framing and intentional next step. Do not invent testimonials, audience metrics, certifications, customer results or income guarantees. Do not expose private data or credentials in AI prompts or public examples.

## Commercial boundary
Free education can lead to coaching, technical review, consulting and knowledge products. Any paid service must define its scope, deliverable, price, timing target and completion check before work begins. An enquiry is not a purchase commitment.

## Relationship to DARKTRONIX
DARKTRONIX 9V LABS is the technical education and practical-skills division within Cortex Intelligence Nexus. Its physical laboratory history supplies practical context; the public direction is education-first. The page does not imply that every repair or diagnostic task can be completed remotely.
