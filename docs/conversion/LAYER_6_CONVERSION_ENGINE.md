# Cortex Intelligence Nexus — Layer 6 Conversion Engine

**Status:** Active  
**Date:** 2026-10-07

## Objective
Turn the Layer 5 knowledge and education system into a clear path from discovery and learning to enquiry, qualification, offer, payment/delivery and follow-up.

## Canonical flow
**Discover → Learn → Identify need → Enquire → Qualify → Offer → Payment / delivery → Follow-up**

## Current public conversion surfaces
- Offers / Book: https://cortex-platforms.netlify.app/offers.html
- Content Engine: https://cortex-platforms.netlify.app/content-engine.html
- DARKTRONIX: https://cortex-platforms.netlify.app/darktronix.html
- WhatsApp enquiry: https://wa.me/2349010251577
- Existing validated commerce path documented in the repository commerce records.

## Layer 6 implementation
The Offers page now exposes three deliberate paths:
1. **Learn** — Content & Education Engine.
2. **Technical skills** — DARKTRONIX technical education, diagnostics and coaching.
3. **Discuss a project** — direct WhatsApp routing for automation, consulting or requirements that need qualification.

## Conversion rules
- Education comes before commercial pressure where appropriate.
- A visitor who is unsure can use one clear WhatsApp support route.
- Qualification happens before custom work is promised.
- Existing prices and payment paths are not silently changed by this layer.
- No income, follower, customer or testimonial claims are invented.
- Google Business identity and public Google fields are outside this layer unless explicitly approved.
- Payment/delivery details remain documented separately and are not duplicated as a new payment system here.

## Layer 7 handoff
The next layer can capture qualified enquiry events, operational state and follow-up data without changing the public identity architecture.
