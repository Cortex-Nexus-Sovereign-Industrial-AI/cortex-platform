- generate review-friendly content and local authority

```

## 6. Review and Trust System

### Trust Capturing Plan
- Ask satisfied clients for short Google reviews
- Ask for product feedback after service completion
- Publish short before/after stories and results
- Add service-specific proof (photos, logs, outcomes)

### Trust Rules
- never fabricate results
- use real case examples
- show process, not hype
- keep claims honest and verifiable

---

## 7. CTA Sequence

### CTA Flow

Attention → Interest → Click → Contact → Trust → Sale
```

### CTA types
- Learn more

- Buy now

## 8. Weekly Platform Routine

## 9. Content Tags / Hooks

Use hooks like:
- “Why this thing broke unexpectedly”
- “This should not have happened”
- “Things people ignore until it is too late”
- “I fixed this in 5 minutes”
- “The real cause was not what everyone thought”
- “This little mistake cost the owner money”

## 10. KPI Targets

### Engagement targets
- CTR from platform to website: > 2%
- average watch time: > 30% of video length
- saves + shares: high priority
- comment engagement: strong community signal

### Conversion targets
- website click → offer click
- offer click → checkout start
- payment completion
- member access grant
