- publish 3 follow-up versions of the best performing hook
- optimize CTA based on click behavior
- refine service page copy

### Week 3 — Offer push
- focus on strongest offer

### Week 4 — Measure and improve

## 5. CTA and conversion flow

### Primary flow
Website / content → offers page → payment or WhatsApp → confirmation → access or service delivery

### Funnel rules
- CTA should be obvious on all posts
- each piece of content should carry one clear next action
- each offer should state the outcome simply
- every purchase route should lead to access or delivery confirmation

## 6. Premium visual design notes

### Design direction
- premium but clean
- dark backdrop + bright accent colors
- highly readable typography
- strong whitespace
- strong CTA contrast
- clean line art or cards
