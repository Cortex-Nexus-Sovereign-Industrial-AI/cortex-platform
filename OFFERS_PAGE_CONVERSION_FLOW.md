## 3. Offer Page Layout

### Header
**Choose the system that fits your next growth move.**

### Product Cards
Each card should include:
- clear title
- brief description
- outcome
- scope
- price
- delivery timeline
- CTA button

### Trust Section
- what you get
- how it works
- examples
- no fake promises

### FAQ Section
Answer common objections:
- Is this for beginners?
- What do I receive?
- How long does it take?
- What if I need help after purchase?

---

## 4. Conversion Rules

- CTA should be visible without scrolling on mobile
- product card price should be repeated visually
- show a small outcome statement under each product
- include a clear guarantee or service expectation
- avoid overload or too much text

---

## 5. Sales Flow

```text
User sees offer
  ↓
Chooses product
  ↓
Reads what is included
  ↓
Clicks CTA
  ↓
Proceed to Paystack or WhatsApp
  ↓
Make payment or request service quote
  ↓
Receive access or confirmation
```
