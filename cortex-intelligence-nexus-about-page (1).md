# About Cortex Intelligence Nexus

## Building intelligent systems with purpose

Cortex Intelligence Nexus Intel Solution is an industrial AI engineering and software automation company founded by **Michael Ujuku Morim** in **Ogoja, Nigeria**.

We design and deploy scalable software systems, autonomous automation architectures, and custom data integrations that help organizations build stronger, more connected digital infrastructure. Our work brings together software engineering, industrial AI, automation, and systems thinking to solve complex technical challenges with practical business value.

## Why we started

Many developers, businesses, and institutions rely on fragmented tools and surface-level applications that solve isolated problems but do not create lasting technical control. This can lead to passive dependency, disconnected systems, and infrastructure that is difficult to understand, adapt, or scale.

Cortex Intelligence Nexus was created to address that challenge.

Our purpose is to help organizations move beyond simply using pre-packaged applications. We build systems that support deeper technical judgment, meaningful automation, strong data integration, and greater control over the digital infrastructure businesses depend on.

From autonomous software automation matrices and proprietary software development kits to decentralized industrial AI solutions, we focus on creating technology that is integrated, adaptable, and built for real-world use.
