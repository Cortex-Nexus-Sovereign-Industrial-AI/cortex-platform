# Weekly Operating Checklist — Cortex Intelligence Nexus
**Purpose:** Turn the shop and digital offers into consistent cash.  
**Updated:** 2026-09-25

Use this every week. Tick the boxes. Focus is finished, paid work.

---

## Daily (Mon–Sat)

- [ ] Open WhatsApp Business / Status with today’s available services
- [ ] Reply to all repair and automation enquiries within 2 hours during shop hours
- [ ] Accept only jobs with diagnosis fee paid (₦2,000–₦5,000) or clear pre-agreed total
- [ ] Decline or re-price any pure multi-day tracing job that has no deposit
- [ ] Log every new job: customer name, model/fault or automation type, amount agreed, payment status
- [ ] Complete and collect payment on at least one job if possible
- [ ] Take one clear photo of any finished set / working automation (for proof and Google review later)

---

## Repair-specific rules

- Diagnosis fee first. Full repair only after customer agrees the total.
- Target net after parts: ₦5,000–₦15,000 per completed set.
- Keep a short list of the 3–4 fastest, highest-success faults you know. Prefer those.
- End of day: count cash / Moniepoint / Opay received from repairs.

---

## Agro / Automation push (minimum 2 times per week)

- [ ] Post or forward the agro automation offer in at least one local WhatsApp group or to 3–5 traders/shops
- [ ] Send the ready quote message (see WHATSAPP_SALES_SCRIPT.md)
- [ ] Follow up any previous quote that has not replied

---

## Digital mid-ticket (when enquiries come)

- [ ] Use the exact scripts in `docs/commerce/WHATSAPP_SALES_SCRIPT.md`
- [ ] Only start work after Paystack confirmation
- [ ] Keep concurrent active 30-day systems to a manageable number (max 5 recommended)

---

## End of week (Sunday or Saturday evening)

- [ ] Total cash received this week (repairs + agro + digital)
- [ ] Number of completed paid jobs
- [ ] Number of Google reviews requested / received
- [ ] Note which faults or automation types paid best
- [ ] Adjust next week’s focus accordingly
