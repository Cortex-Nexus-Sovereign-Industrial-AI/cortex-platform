## 1. Weekly Aim

Keep the business active, visible, and improving through a tight weekly operating cycle.

---

## 2. Weekly Tasks

### Monday
- review metrics dashboard
- review website and offer flow
- confirm social plans
- prepare 3 content ideas

### Tuesday
- produce and publish 2 educational clips
- update Google Business profile
- schedule 2 platform posts

### Wednesday
- produce and publish one longer video
- share one service or offer update
- review comments and lead responses

### Thursday
- publish one practical “problem solved” video
- push clear CTA to WhatsApp or offers page

### Friday
- share a meme or humor-led post with educational value
- collect review / client feedback
- track conversion performance

### Saturday
- publish a recap or result-based post
- review engagement and identify best hook

### Sunday
- analyze week metrics
- identify what was most effective
- update next week’s content plan

---

## 3. Core Weekly KPIs

- website clicks
- offer clicks
- WhatsApp contacts
- Paystack conversions
- access grants created
- top-performing content piece
- best source of leads

---

## 4. Weekly Decision Rules

- if one content type clearly converts, repeat it
- if one hook performs well, repurpose it in 3 formats
- if a CTA underperforms, rewrite it
- if Google Business is quiet, post a local update
- if a service page is underused, improve clarity and pricing

---

## 5. Quarterly Review

- identify which offer is strongest
- which content type drives the most revenue
- which platform creates most qualified leads
- which system needs improvement

---

**This checklist keeps the business active, measurable, and improvement-driven.**
