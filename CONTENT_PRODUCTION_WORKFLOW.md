## 1. Workflow Objective

Create a content engine that is:
- funny
- educational
- highly shareable
- directly linked to buying or booking behavior
- easy to scale across multiple media channels

---

## 2. Production Flow

```text
Idea capture
  ↓
Hook writing
  ↓
Problem + lesson + punchline
  ↓
Demo or show-and-tell
  ↓
Edit for platform format
  ↓
CTA insertion
  ↓
Upload + schedule
  ↓
Review performance
  ↓
Repurpose winning content
```

---

## 3. Content Formula

### Formula 1: Funny + Practical
```
"This thing was acting weird for 3 days… it was not the power. Here’s the actual problem and the fix."
```

### Formula 2: Story + Lesson
```
"I saw this mistake again today. It looked innocent. It almost destroyed the equipment. Here is why."
```

### Formula 3: Meme + Fix
```
Image or meme with visual irony + short diagnostic explanation + simple fix + CTA
```

### Formula 4: Behind-the-Scenes + Authority
```
Show the real work, explain what you saw, narrate the logic, then present the practical lesson.
```

---

## 4. Weekly Production Model

### 1 long-form educational video
- explains a real problem
- includes practical solution

### 3 short educational reels
- solves common issues
- built for retention

### 2 meme-driven explanation videos
- funny, relatable, punchy

### 1 CTA-driven conversion post
- clear offer or service link

### 1 Google Business update
- local relevance + social proof

---

## 5. Subject Categories

- electronics fault diagnosis
- practical tech tips
- shop/trader automation problems
- weird everyday device issues
- “don’t do this” warnings
- business automation lessons
- real human story + technical fix

---

## 6. CTA Placement Rules

### Good CTA placements
- opening or first 5 seconds for strong authority hooks
- end screen for action
- pinned comments
- captions with visible website / WhatsApp contact

### CTA examples
- Watch the full breakdown
- Book a diagnosis
- Learn the system
- Get the 30-Day AI Content System
- DM for quote

---

## 7. Repurposing System

One core idea should become multiple assets:
- YouTube long-form
- YouTube short
- TikTok short
- Reels version
- X thread or post
- Google Business update
- blog/FAQ snippet

This ensures a single campaign generates multiple revenue touchpoints.

---

## 8. Notes on Tone

Keep tone:
- warm
- practical
- humorous
- educational
- persuasive without hype

Avoid:
- fake hype
- exaggerated outcomes
- “guaranteed income” claims
- manipulative language
- childish or low-quality meme culture that undermines professionalism

---

## 9. High-Performance Content Checklist

- [ ] clear hook in first 2 seconds
- [ ] real problem stated clearly
- [ ] short practical lesson
- [ ] obvious value to viewer
- [ ] CTA included naturally
- [ ] platform-appropriate format
- [ ] text overlay used for retention
- [ ] share-worthy moment included

---

## 10. Production Priority

### Highest Priority Content Types
1. problem solved with a practical lesson
2. funny but technically useful short videos
3. before-and-after service stories
4. educational myth-busting clips
5. behind-the-scenes operator work
