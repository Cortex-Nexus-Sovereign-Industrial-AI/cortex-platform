# MEME + EDUCATIONAL CONTENT FRAMEWORK

**Effective:** 2026-10-09

---

## 1. Objective

Create content that is:
- funny enough to be shared
- educational enough to be trusted
- specific enough to solve a real problem
- direct enough to generate leads and sales

This framework blends humor, teaching, and practical logic.

---

## 2. Content Core Principle

**funny explanation + useful lesson + practical fix**

The meme should not just be a joke. It must carry a teaching moment.

---

## 3. Content Archetypes

### Archetype 1: “This thing is doing too much”
- device behaving oddly
- funny visual tension
- short explanation
- lesson for prevention or repair

### Archetype 2: “Why did this happen?”
- problem framing
- root cause
- real-world answer
- CTA to service or product

### Archetype 3: “People ignore this and regret it”
- relatable scenario
- practical mistake explained
- humor + correction

### Archetype 4: “The technician’s reaction”
- use direct, funny narrative
- show expertise in a digestible format
- CTA to book diagnosis or training

---

## 4. Script Pattern

```text
Hook: "This thing had me sweating for 3 days..."
Problem: explain what happened
Reveal: the root cause
Funny line: punchline or reaction
Lesson: what to do / what to avoid
CTA: Watch full story / book service / buy the system
```

---

## 5. Safe Audience Standards

Content should be:
- safe for general audience
- non-abusive
- light-hearted
- teaching-focused
- family-friendly humor

Do not push:
- false promises
- aggressive sales claims
- shame-based content
- inappropriate or toxic jokes

---

## 6. Example Content Hook Pool

- “This little mistake cost the owner 3 days of stress”
- “The device was not broken — it was just tired”
- “This thing was trying to tell me something…”
- “I saw this mistake again. It looked small. It was not.”
- “The real problem was not the part — it was the setup.”
- “This should have been obvious but nobody checked it.”

---

## 7. Educational Value Layer

Every meme must carry at least one of these:
- a practical lesson
- a diagnosis clue
- a prevention strategy
- a business insight
- a tool/approach recommendation

---

Humor drives attention. Education builds trust. Value creates purchases.
