## 1. Objective

Track content performance across all channels and tie them directly to conversion and revenue.

This is not vanity tracking. It is operational signal analysis.

## 4. KPI Definitions

### Click Through Rate (CTR)
```text
CTR = offer_clicks / views
```

### Conversion Rate
```text
Conversion Rate = purchases / offer_clicks
```

### Revenue per Content Piece
```text
RPM = revenue_generated / content_pieces_published
```

### Retention Rate
```text
Retention = average_watch_time / video_length
```

## 6. Source Attribution

Every purchase should be tied to a content source.

Example:
- TikTok Reel → website CTA → Paystack purchase
- YouTube video → WhatsApp lead → repair job
- Google Business review → service enquiry → manual sale

This makes the revenue source traceable and measurable.

## 8. Use in Revenue System

The dashboard should tie together:

This closes the loop between attention and business result.

---

## 9. Quick Optimization Rules

- If a content piece has high views but low CTR → fix CTA
- If high CTR but low purchase → fix offer design or trust signal
- If high shares and saves → expand that content theme
- If low watch time → tighten hook and pacing
