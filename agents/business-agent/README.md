# Business Agent

This folder supports operational analysis, insight generation, and premium business content.

## Goal
Turn repo data, offers, member activity, and platform operations into useful business intelligence.

## Typical outputs
- monthly insight report
- offer optimization recommendations
- conversion analysis notes
- customer/value reflection summaries
- member education improvements

## Best fit for
- enterprise offers
- member dashboards
- service refinement
- metrics-based decision making

## Rule
Business content must not promise guaranteed revenue or success. It should instead explain operational reasoning and value creation clearly.
